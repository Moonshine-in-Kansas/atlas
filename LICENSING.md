**ATLAS Research and Attribution License 1.0**, in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
It permits noncommercial research under its conditions, requires scholarly
attribution and citation, and requires separate written permission for commercial
use and AI training, including noncommercial training. Distributed derivative works
of covered material must include the complete license and preserve its conditions.

## Existing permissions and separately licensed material

This change is not retroactive. Versions previously offered under Apache 2.0,
including tag `v0.1.0`, retain that licensing history. Valid permissions previously
granted are not revoked; substantially the same material may remain obtainable
under the earlier license. The new default does not eliminate that alternative.

The ATLAS-owned source notices identify the current license. Existing copyright
and authorship attributions are preserved. Historical license texts remain in the
corresponding Git revisions.

Lean, mathlib and other dependencies retain their respective licenses. Third-party
material, including any incorporated portions, is not relicensed by this change.
The custom license applies only to rights the licensor has authority to grant.

Only the license-comment lines in 109 Lean files changed; mathematical
code, pins and historical verification artifacts are unchanged. Historical audit evidence continues to refer
to its recorded source snapshot. This notice is not a new build or proof audit.
