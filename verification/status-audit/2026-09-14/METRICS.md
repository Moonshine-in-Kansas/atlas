## Proof footprints

CODE_COUNTS.csv uses 36 selected compiled roots: one order and one simplicity theorem for each of 18 entries. Each closure traverses constants in types and values (including opaque values, implicit instances, and inductive/recursor metadata). Each recoverable declaration range is assigned to the compiler module of its **range parent**, which can differ from the module of a generated helper. Nested/generated helpers without a range inherit the nearest named parent range. End positions at column zero exclude that final line. Each physical source line is counted once per union.

Ranges include statement, proof/definition body, and interior comments/blank lines. They generally exclude imports and preceding documentation. These are dependency footprints, not just the final wrapper's length. They are not measures of mathematical difficulty, human effort, executable complexity, or complete foundational proof size.
